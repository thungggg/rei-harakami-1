Who is Rei Harakami?

Rei Harakami was a legendary Japanese electronic musician. He was known for his unique blend of ambient but punchy electronic music. He was apart of the late 90s and early 2000s Japanese electronic music scene, his calm yet exciting rhythems were a breath of fresh air in the scene.

Harakami smoking a cigarette.

Life

Harakami was born in Hiroshima Japan, but later moved to Kyoto. Not much is known about his early life, but it's known that he attended the Kyoto University of Arts to become a filmmaker. However he started to gradually shift his focus towards music, and got signed to a Sublime records in 1996. On July 27th, 2011, Harakami passed away at the age of 40 due to a brain hemorrhage.
